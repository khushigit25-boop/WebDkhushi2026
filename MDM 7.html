College Department FAQ

IT25084

1. What are the college working hours?
College working hours are generally from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
2. How can I apply for a leave?
Students can apply for leave through the college portal or by submitting a leave application to the department.
3. Where can I find the examination timetable?
The examination timetable is available on the college website and department notice board.
4. How can I contact my department?
You can contact the department office during working hours or email the department coordinator.
5. How can I get study materials?
Study materials are provided by faculty members through the college LMS or classroom groups.
6. What should I do if I forget my student ID?
Contact the department office or college administration for help.
7. How can I check my attendance?
Students can check their attendance through the student portal or by contacting the respective faculty member.
8. Where can I get information about placements?
Placement information is provided by the Training and Placement Cell through notices, emails and the placement portal.
